# Prototype v0.4 — Long-term Training, Running and Pull-up Expansion

This amendment records the approved expansion from an eight-week tracker into a long-term home-training system.

## Programme model

- Week 8 completes Block 1 rather than ending the programme.
- Six connected eight-week blocks plus a four-week annual review preserve history across 12 months.
- The user explicitly accepts each next block.
- Default track: Lean Athletic. Alternatives: Muscle Emphasis and Hybrid Runner–Strength.

## Pull-up bar

- The pull-up bar is a first-class equipment item.
- Workout C replaces its duplicate one-arm row with a vertical-pull progression. Horizontal rows remain elsewhere in the week.
- Pull-up progression has four rungs: hang/scapular control, controlled negatives, assisted repetitions and strict pull-ups.
- The user chooses a rung; the app never assumes full pull-ups.
- The app displays mounting and pre-session safety checks and does not prescribe kipping.
- Block 1 uses one weekly pull-up exposure. Block 2 increases to two when recovery is acceptable.

## Running

- Weeks 1–2 use strength and walking only.
- From Week 3, one weekly easy run–walk session becomes first-class programme data.
- Initial template: 1 minute easy running, 2 minutes walking, repeated eight times.
- Later blocks may add a second easy run after recovery review.
- GPS and route tracking remain outside this prototype.

## Week 8 review

- Reviews strength sessions, pull-up rung, easy-run completion, weight, waist, meals and readiness.
- Gives one recommendation and requires confirmation before Block 2.
- Sets realistic visual expectations: moderate visible improvement, not a dramatic or guaranteed transformation.

## Prototype boundary

All new state is simulated in memory. Production persistence, service workers, IndexedDB, notifications and migrations remain intentionally unimplemented.
