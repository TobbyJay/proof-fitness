# Code of Conduct

Proof Fitness contributors are expected to create a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

## Expected behaviour

- Use inclusive and considerate language.
- Respect different bodies, abilities, cultures, and training backgrounds.
- Give actionable technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Treat health, weight, food, and body-image discussions with care.
- Respect privacy and do not request unnecessary personal health information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised conduct, deliberate humiliation, and publishing another person’s private information are not tolerated.

Project maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
